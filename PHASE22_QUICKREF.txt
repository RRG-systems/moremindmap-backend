COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID
================================================================================

❌ Trading tight spreads (< 50 bps)
   → Spreads don't cover slippage
   → Focus on 100+ bps EMERGING

❌ Holding EMERGING > 10 days
   → Spreads compress, viability drops
   → Exit or move to MID stage rules

❌ Ignoring position limits
   → Too much capital in one stage = portfolio risk
   → Keep 60% EMERGING, 25% MID, 15% MATURE

❌ No stops (just hoping)
   → Prediction markets can gap instantly
   → Always set 2.5x spread as stop loss

❌ Overweighting new markets (< 1 day old)
   → Extra volatile, most spread captured in day 2-3
   → Wait until day 1 complete before trading
