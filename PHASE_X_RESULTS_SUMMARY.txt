ANSWER: YES, but only at scale with sophisticated execution.

- Spread available: 1.5-2 bps
- Cost per round-trip (maker): 4 bps (2 bps fee × 2 sides)
- Net spread capture: -2.5 bps per round (UNPROFITABLE alone)

BUT with VIP rebates (0-1 bps maker fee):

Risks:
- Price movement wipes out spread profit (if 10 bps move, spread profit erased)
- Requires hedging or smaller positions
- Execution risk (slippage on entry/exit)

Verdict:
- Pure spread capture: BARELY PROFITABLE (0.5-2 bps per round)
- Realistic application: Combine with directional signal
- Better strategy: Use spread as additional revenue layer
