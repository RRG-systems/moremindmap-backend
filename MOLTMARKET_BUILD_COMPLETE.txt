================================================================================
EXECUTION MODEL
================================================================================

All three layers receive the SAME SIGNAL.
Difference is in FILL QUALITY and COSTS.

Paper (Idealized):
- Entry: Best bid/ask + 0-2 bps slippage
- Exit: Best bid/ask + 0-2 bps slippage
- Cost: ~0-2 bps round-trip
- Purpose: Test signal quality without friction

Shadow (Realistic):
- Entry: Ask +3 bps (longs) or Bid -3 bps (shorts) + 2-5 bps slippage
- Exit: Opposite side degradation
- Cost: 10 bps round-trip total
- Purpose: Estimate real-world execution cost

Backtest (Historical):
- Entry/Exit: Same as shadow (realistic fills)
- Data: 45-day historical walk-forward
- Purpose: Reference against past performance

   Rolling metrics snapshot (optional, for trend analysis)

4. execution_quality.csv

            expected_exit, shadow_exit, slippage_bps, paper_shadow_delta
   Execution analysis (optional, for cost tracking)
