# MAKE_MONEY_SUGGESTIONS.md

**Persistent file for profit improvement observations from ROCKY sessions.**

Every session, ROCKY captures observations about:
- What's preventing profitability
- What BRAIN rules should be tightened
- What Nursery mutation types are weak
- What market conditions hurt us
- What execution behavior bleeds edge
- What strategy families look promising
- What calibration improved or hurt results
- Profitable strategy ideas we're missing

---

## Format

Each entry follows:
```
TIMESTAMP: [ISO timestamp]
CATEGORY: [BRAIN_ACCELERATION | BRAIN_DECELERATION | STRATEGY_DECAY | MARKET_PROBLEM | EXECUTION_BLEED | NURSERY_QUALITY | PROFIT_IDEA | USER_TESTING]
OBSERVATION: [what we noticed]
WHY_THIS_MATTERS: [profit impact]
SUGGESTION: [concrete action]
CONFIDENCE: [0.0-1.0]
```
